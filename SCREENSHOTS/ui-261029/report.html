  • HudView.LayoutProblems() now flags any visible button or badge smaller than 48 screen pixels either way (HudView.MinTapPx). At 1080x2340 today, segment buttons are 237x78 px, tabs 315x92 and icon buttons 102x102.
  • HudLayoutTests lays every screen out on 5 phones (1080x1920, 1080x2340, 1170x2532, 1440x3200, 1080x2520), up from 3.

48 px is a floor against regressions, not the Material 48 dp guideline. On a 1080-wide phone, 78 px is about 30 dp, so the segment buttons are still below that guideline.
